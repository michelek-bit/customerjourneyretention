**0:00 · Title · From one booking to two**  
Meet Maria. She's a C S R at Home Alliance, and she answers about forty calls a day. This is the story of how OutboundOS turns those calls into more bookings, and more money in her pocket.

**0:11 · Attention · The missed chance**  
Here's the problem. Most calls end with one job booked, and nothing more. On the Master tab, the funnel shows it clearly: hundreds of bookings where no one offered a second service.

**0:22 · Attention · Where we stand**  
The benchmark shows where we stand against the industry. Right below, the goal tracker shows if we are on pace for the month, and exactly how many bookings we need each day.

**0:33 · Interest · A call comes in**  
Now watch Maria take a call. The phone rings, and OutboundOS matches the caller to the property record: an eighteen-year-old home. It knows which systems are due, which approved offers fit, and what the call could be worth.

**0:46 · Interest · Know the customer**  
Customer three sixty opens with the full history, so Maria never asks the same question twice. The A I brief tells her what to say first.

**0:55 · Interest · A coach on every call**  
As she talks, Live Coach suggests the next best move, and highlights objections as they happen. The rule is simple: help first, recommend second, book third.

**1:05 · Desire · What’s in it for you: ₱100 each**  
So what's in it for Maria? During the Cash Grab, every qualified cross-booking pays one hundred pesos, paid daily, with no limit. Just two extra a day adds about four thousand four hundred pesos in a twenty-two day month.

**1:19 · Desire · A new contest every day**  
Every day brings a new contest with its own reward: double pesos, mystery envelopes, early outs and team parties. And on Black Friday, it's triple pesos, plus a bonus for the top C S R.

**1:32 · Desire · Points for great conversations**  
Handling objections well earns points too. Each touchpoint lights up on screen. Maria banks her points after the call, and trades them for coffee, an early out, G Cash, or a movie night.

**1:45 · Desire · Get recognized**  
The leaderboard puts top performers in the spotlight, so great work gets noticed by the whole team, and by leadership.

**1:52 · Desire · Rewards that fit you**  
And rewards fit the person. The C S R DNA questionnaire learns what motivates Maria, like saving for a home or more time with family, and matches prizes and contest days to her goals. Daily progress shows how close she is.

**2:06 · Desire · Pip has your back**  
Pip, the A I helper, cheers every win, and answers questions in one tap, like what today's contest is, or how close you are to your goal.

**2:15 · Action · Start today in 4 steps**  
Here's how to start today. One: take calls in SmartTool with the coach. Two: log every cross-booking in Cash Grab with its job number. Three: fill in your C S R DNA. Four: check your daily progress, and ask Pip anytime.

**2:30 · Action · Leaders: follow the rollout**
