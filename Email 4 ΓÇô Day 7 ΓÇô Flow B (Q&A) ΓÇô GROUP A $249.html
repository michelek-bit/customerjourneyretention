How often, what it costs, and what happens if we find a problem.

How often does a built-in refrigerator need maintenance?
Sub-Zero recommends cleaning the condenser every 3 to 6 months and replacing water and air filters about every 6 months.
What does the Care Visit cost?
$249 for the full visit. Parts, filters and repairs are quoted before any work.
What if you find a problem?
You get a photo, an explanation and a written quote. You decide.
Can you clean the ice maker too?
Yes. We deep clean and sanitize it for $149 on the same visit.
What can it cost if I skip maintenance?
A fan motor replacement typically runs $350–$750 and a compressor or sealed-system repair $1,200–$2,500. Replacing a built-in refrigerator costs $8,000–$18,000. A clean condenser coil can also make the refrigerator up to 30% more efficient, and if it stops cooling, perishable food must be thrown out after 4 hours.
