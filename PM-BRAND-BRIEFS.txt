HOW THE SUGGESTIONS WERE BUILT
THREE FILTERS BEHIND EVERY SUGGESTED PRICE
1 · Market
LA/OC competitor prices for the same service (checked Oct 22, 2026); national benchmarks only where no local price exists.
2 · Clock
Maintenance period from the manufacturer or a safety body (Sub-Zero, DOE, Navien, NFPA, NADCA, Generac) sets the reminder timing.
3 · Elasticity
Bookings change = (new ÷ old price)^E. Raise where |E| < 1 (high-end), hold where |E| ≈ 1, price to market where |E| > 1.
Elasticities are estimates until each brand runs a 50/50 price test in Customer.io. All prices need Ops margin approval before launch.
Full detail: High-End Preventive Maintenance Review deck and PM review workbook.
Use this slide only if a manager asks how prices were set.
