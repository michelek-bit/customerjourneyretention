NEW TRANSACTIONAL SET — SMS COPY (Customer.io)
Upsell + cross-book proposal · 2026-10-21

V1 = locations with 2+ trades (cross-book other departments). V2 = single-trade locations (upsell more services from the same trade).
SMS 1 = plain update for everyone. Offer / cross-sell SMS = only contacts with SMS marketing consent, STOP line, quiet hours 9 AM-8 PM. Follow-ups fire only if the customer has not acted. GSM-7 only (hyphen, no star/emoji). Brand name first.
