# 4C Retention CRM · Onboarding SOP

How to find every piece of information in the 4C Retention Ecosystem app. In the app: **START HERE** (top bar) or the address ending `#start`. Narrated video tour: `video/onboarding.mp4` (about 3 min 50 s, sound on).

## SOP · 6 steps to find anything

1. **Pick the project.** The dark bar at the very top switches between the three projects: 4C RETENTION (keep customers), 4C PROSPECTING (win new ones) and AI-OFFICE (the AI agents that run both). START HERE brings you back to this page.
2. **Pick the tab or section.** 4C RETENTION uses the tab bar under the top switch. 4C PROSPECTING uses the left sidebar. AI-OFFICE uses Office · Map · Dashboard · List at the top of the game.
3. **Narrow down with sub-tabs and filters.** Most tabs have sub-tabs (for example Non-Member › Weekly workflow) and a channel list (Print, Email, SMS, In-app, Retargeting, Landing page). Touchpoint rails (0–9) narrow the list to one moment in the journey.
4. **Open the item.** Click any message, touchpoint, card or row: the real email renders in the preview, with subject, timing, audience, offer, merge tokens and open issues on the right.
5. **Act on it.** Use the built-in controls: edit Calculator inputs, run AI dispatch, change P&L assumptions, drag pipeline cards. Edits save in your browser only; Reset returns to the source sheet.
6. **Can’t find it? Ask.** Use “Hi! Ask me anything”, or the table below. Every row has a Go button that takes you straight there. Share a direct link with the address ending (for example #members).

## House rules

- **Sample vs real:** Anything marked “sample” or “example” is illustrative. Numbers from your sheets and Customer.io exports say where they came from.
- **Colors:** Amber = needs a person to decide · Red = blocker · Green = automated / done.
- **Versions:** V1 = locations with 2+ trades (cross-book) · V2 = single-trade locations (upsell). “New” = proposed redesign; the others are today’s live versions.
- **Your edits:** Calculator, quests and assumptions are saved in this browser only. Nobody else sees them.
- **Sharing:** Copy the page address with its ending (#calculator, #members, #non-member, #personas, #prospecting, #tech-assignment, #ai-office, #inhouse-figma, #d7, #start) to send someone to that exact place.
- **Owners:** MK accountable · Victor: AI product · Diego: omnichannel (email, SMS, ads, pages) · Nazar: data sourcing. Each has an AI agent in AI-OFFICE.
